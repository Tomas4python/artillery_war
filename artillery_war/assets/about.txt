ABOUT THE APP: ARTILLERY WAR, 21ST CENTURY, EASTERN FRONT, EUROPE

---

Introduction:
Artillery War is an engaging strategic simulation set in the unstable environment of 21st century Europe, casting you into the heart of fierce artillery battles on the Eastern Front. Crafted with diligence by Tomas Suslavicius, this game brings forward the gravity of war, the importance of strategic decision-making, and the unpredictability of warfare. Whether you’re the defender holding your ground or the intruder breaking through enemy lines, every decision matters.

---

Purpose:
The inception of "Artillery War" isn't just for entertainment; it serves as a beacon of Tomas Suslavicius's journey in mastering Python coding. With every line of code and every tactical scenario, the game reflects a blend of passion for game development and the intricacies of programming.

Requirements:
This game is forged on Windows and also runs on Linux, with a range of modules and tools. Here’s a brief glimpse of the technological marvel behind the scenes:
- Core Libraries: `tkinter`, `customtkinter`, `PIL` (Pillow), `pygame`, `math`, `random`, `dataclasses`, `pathlib`, and `ctypes` (Windows only).
- Application Modules (package `artillery_war`):
  - `main.py` - Launch file.
  - `config.py` - Game balance constants and player options.
  - `core/campaign.py` - The war: resources between battles, maps, weather and the final verdict.
  - `core/battle.py` - The heart of the game: turns, the computer's fire and battle outcomes.
  - `core/ballistics.py` - Shell trajectory, wind and blast damage computations.
  - `core/units.py` - Definition and deployment of game units.
  - `ui/app.py` - Main window, menus and reports.
  - `ui/controller.py` - Runs battles in the windows and plays out each turn.
  - `ui/console.py` - The commander's field console.
  - `ui/map_view.py` - Drone view of the battlefield.
  - `ui/radar_view.py` - Real-time tracking and targeting system.
  - `ui/tablet.py` - The military tablet look of the console and reports.
  - `platform_utils.py` - Differences between Windows and Linux.
- Additional Documents: `about.txt`, `rules.txt` and `changelog.txt` for comprehensive game info.
Note. If display screen is scaled (e.g. to 125%), the game may not run in full screen mode or may contain distortions, the game was designed for screen scale 100% and resolution not less than 1920x1080.
